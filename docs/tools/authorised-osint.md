# Authorised OSINT Toolkit

**Review an organization’s public exposure**

Build a defensible view of an authorized organization’s public assets, email-domain posture, and exposed services.

**Works well with:** Adversarial Verifier, Reverse Engineering Researcher, Research Evaluator, Infrastructure Reliability Engineer.

## What to bring

- Scope and domains
- Known asset list
- Evidence requirements

## Setup

1. Define the owned or authorized organization, domains, allowed sources, and the assessment question.

**Access:** Public sources; optional providers may require accounts.

## Supported functions

| Function | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| `org-attack-surface` | Inspect an authorized organization’s public assets |
| `email-domain-security` | Review email-domain configuration |
| `cloud-saas-exposure` | Review relevant cloud and SaaS exposure |

## Workflow

1. Confirm the assessment scope and collect public asset references.
2. Check the relevant domain, email, cloud, and service exposure.
3. Corroborate important findings with direct records and retrieval dates.
4. Prioritize repairs by evidence, likely impact, and the responsible asset owner.

## What you get

- Public exposure inventory
- Source-backed findings
- Remediation priorities

## Check the result

- Every finding concerns an authorized asset and retains its source.
- High-impact findings have corroborating evidence and a practical next action.
