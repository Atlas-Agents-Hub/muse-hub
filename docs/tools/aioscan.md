# AIOScan: AI Visibility Check

**Make public pages easier for AI systems to read**

Check whether a public page is accessible to AI crawlers and whether its content and metadata are clear.

**Works well with:** Product Interface Designer, Infrastructure Reliability Engineer, Creative Studio.

## What to bring

- Public URL
- Important page content

## Setup

1. Choose the public page to inspect and retain access to its source or site settings.

**Access:** Provider access.

## Supported functions

| Function | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| `scan_website` | Check page access, content, and AI-readability findings |

## Workflow

1. Scan the page and retain the report URL.
2. Inspect failed fetch, content, robots, title, heading, and structured-data checks.
3. Fix the owning page template or site setting.
4. Scan the same URL again and compare the individual checks.

## What you get

- Visibility report
- Specific page fixes
- Comparison of checks

## Check the result

- The intended content is present in the fetched page.
- Fixed findings improve on a repeated scan of the same URL.
