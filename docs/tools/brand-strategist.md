# Brand Strategist

**Make the product promise clear and consistent**

Develop a concise brand strategy from real audience needs, product benefits, positioning, and a consistent voice.

**Works well with:** Creative Studio, Product Interface Designer, Coordinator.

## What to bring

- Audience and problem
- Product strengths
- Tone and reference examples

## Setup

1. Gather the product description, audience, examples, and approved business facts.

**Access:** Provider access.

## Supported functions

| Function | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| `get_brand_framework` | Load the strategy questions and presentation structure |
| `validate_brand_strategy` | Check approved wording against the presentation frames |
| `render_brand_presentation` | Render the strategy as an editable presentation |

## Workflow

1. Load the strategy framework and answer its sections using the supplied facts.
2. Resolve contradictions between the audience, promise, and reasons to believe.
3. Review the wording and validate it against the presentation layout.
4. Render the approved strategy and use its voice rules in the website and onboarding copy.

## What you get

- Brand strategy
- Voice and messaging rules
- Editable presentation

## Check the result

- Every product claim has a supplied fact or example.
- The approved text fits the presentation without truncation.
