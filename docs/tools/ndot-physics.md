# nDot Physics Research

**Trace a physics claim through its derivation**

Read a published nDot research result together with its assumptions, equations, versions, and review history.

**Works well with:** Research Evaluator, Claims Adjudicator, Learning Coach, Model Builder.

## What to bring

- Research question
- Topic or equation identifier
- Comparison sources when relevant

## Setup

1. Identify the topic or equation to investigate and the comparison question.

**Access:** Published research access.

## Supported functions

| Function | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| `listtopics` | Find published research topics |
| `getderivation` | Read a versioned derivation |
| `getequation` | Follow an equation and its references |
| `getreview` | Read a review for the specific derivation version |

## Workflow

1. Find the relevant topic and research package.
2. Read the question, assumptions, and full derivation for the selected version.
3. Follow referenced equations and inspect reviews attached to that same version.
4. Compare the reasoning with independent literature and state the result at the level supported by the evidence.

## What you get

- Versioned derivation summary
- Equation and assumption map
- Comparison notes

## Check the result

- Equation references and review versions match the analyzed derivation.
- The summary distinguishes the project’s result from independent confirmation.
