# Get started with Muse Hub

Bring a goal and build from a useful first result. Follow a guided route to [organize your day](journeys/ORGANIZE_YOUR_DAY.md), [build and automate](journeys/BUILD_AND_AUTOMATE.md), or [create something](journeys/CREATE_SOMETHING.md). To start directly with a specialist, use the steps below.

## 1. Choose your specialist

[Browse the 15 specialists](DOWNLOAD_A_SPECIALIST.md) and download the one closest to your work. For example, choose Software Engineer for a failing test, Creative Studio for a visual project, or Personal Companion for planning and notes.

Each ZIP includes a `START_HERE.md`, role instructions, selected skills, workflows, and examples. No Python is needed to use these instructions with Muse.

## 2. Open the package in Muse

Extract the ZIP into its own folder. Give Muse the `START_HERE.md`, or attach the ZIP, with a request such as:

> Use this specialist for my current task. Follow its setup guide, keep my existing preferences and memory, and begin with the files and context I provide.

Follow the guide to configure a separate assistant for the role. Add the Main instructions and its three subchats. Use Main to keep the project goal and decisions together; use the subchats for focused parts of the work.

## 3. Try your first task

Every [specialist guide](DOWNLOAD_A_SPECIALIST.md) includes a first-task prompt. Replace the example with your own goal, source material, desired result, and any constraints. For a software issue, include the failing command; for research, include your question and sources; for a design, include the audience and target screen size.

Ask for a result you can inspect: a patch and passing check, an evidence table, a design you can open, or a plan you can act on. [Workroom guidance](handbook/subchat-workrooms.md) helps with longer projects.

## Add what the task needs

- [Skills](SKILLS.md) provide methods for specific tasks; [558 task starters](../collections/muse-starters-v1/CATALOG.md) bundle a useful selection.
- [Workflows](WORKFLOWS.md) connect steps such as research, implementation, review, and handoff.
- [Connectors](connectors/README.md) link to setup instructions for 273 services. Choose your account and requested permissions during that service's setup.
- [Terminal](TERMINAL.md) covers personal Muse chat and the toolkit's local commands.
- [Companion Home](COMPANION_HOME.md) offers five rooms, a notebook, Studio motion controls, and an original Arcade game. [Daily Desk](spaces/DAILY_DESK.md) combines tasks and notes.

## Keep your work

Store your customized instructions, memory, and task files in your private workspace. Keep the downloaded package as a reference. When updating, compare the new instructions with your edits before replacing them. For local notebooks, keep the SQLite database outside the application folder and back it up before moving computers.
