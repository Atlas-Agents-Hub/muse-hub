## Open your package

1. Download the ZIP and extract it into a new folder.
2. Open `START_HERE.md`, or give Muse the ZIP and your current task.
3. Follow the included guide for Main and the three subchats, then try the specialist's first task.

No Python is needed for assistant instructions. Optional local tools list their requirements and commands in the package. Keep your working files and personal memory in your own private workspace.
