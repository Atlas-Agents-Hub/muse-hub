## Start a useful task

- [Lecture Navigator](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/lecture-navigator/STARTER.md) — Map a lecture into its major mechanisms, relationships and exam-relevant distinctions.
- [Source-Based Flashcard Coach](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/flashcard-coach/STARTER.md) — Create answerable, atomic cards that stay inside the learner’s chosen sources.
- [Exam Preparation Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/exam-planner/STARTER.md) — Allocate limited study time to the actual exam scope and observed gaps.
- [Mathematical Reasoning Coach](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/math-coach/STARTER.md) — Help the learner understand a mathematical step and test it on a nearby case.
- [Language Practice Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/language-practice/STARTER.md) — Provide level-appropriate conversation and targeted correction without taking over the learner’s turn.
- [Programming Concepts Tutor](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/programming-tutor/STARTER.md) — Teach programming through the learner’s code and small observable examples.
- [Anesthesia Lecture Study Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/anesthesia-study-coach/STARTER.md) — Organize anesthesia coursework from supplied teaching materials without substituting patient-care guidance.
- [Analytical Reading Companion](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/reading-companion/STARTER.md) — Help a reader understand an argument while distinguishing the author’s claims from interpretation.
- [Study Session Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/study-accountability/STARTER.md) — Make a study block concrete and review actual progress without pressure or invented reminders.
- [Concept Explanation Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/concept-explainer/STARTER.md) — Clarify a difficult distinction with accurate examples and a meaningful check of understanding.
- [Interview Rehearsal Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/interview-coach/STARTER.md) — Help a person explain their work with specific evidence under realistic follow-up.
- [Technical Storytelling Coach](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/technical-story-coach/STARTER.md) — Translate technical decisions into a clear explanation suited to the listener.
