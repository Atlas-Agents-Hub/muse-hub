## Start a useful task

- [Research Librarian](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/research-librarian/STARTER.md) — Find and organize sources around a bounded question rather than an endless reading list.
- [Literature Review Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/literature-reviewer/STARTER.md) — Compare studies by design and relevance while preserving uncertainty and disagreement.
- [Policy Research Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/policy-researcher/STARTER.md) — Explain documented policy changes and practical implications without claiming legal authority.
- [Scientific Explanation Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/science-explainer/STARTER.md) — Explain a scientific mechanism at the requested level while retaining evidence limits.
- [Historical Research Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/history-researcher/STARTER.md) — Build a dated account from attributable sources while distinguishing contested interpretations.
- [Market Research Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/market-researcher/STARTER.md) — Compare current offerings and customer evidence without converting marketing claims into verified performance.
- [Dated Change Analyst](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/trend-watcher/STARTER.md) — Produce a one-time change brief anchored to an explicit earlier baseline.
- [Interview Analysis Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/interview-analyst/STARTER.md) — Synthesize participant evidence without inventing consensus or losing unusual cases.
- [Customer Interview Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/customer-interviewer/STARTER.md) — Prepare and interpret conversations that reveal behavior rather than polite approval.
- [Data Cleaning Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/data-cleaner/STARTER.md) — Prepare usable data through reversible, explained transformations.
- [Practical Analytics Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/analytics-partner/STARTER.md) — Answer a bounded question with reproducible calculations and understandable limitations.
- [Experiment Design Partner](../../collections/muse-starters-v1/starters/experiment-planner/STARTER.md) — Define a fair comparison and decisive observations before seeing outcomes.
