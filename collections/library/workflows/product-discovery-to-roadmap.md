# Turn Product Research into a Roadmap

Convert customer and market evidence into a focused product sequence.

## Bring these inputs

- Product question and target users
- Available customer and market evidence

## 1. Market Research

Read [the Market Research method](../skills/market-research/SKILL.md). Collect the evidence relevant to the product question and alternatives.

**Carry forward:** A market and customer evidence brief.

**Check:** Sources, dates, and uncertainty are visible for consequential findings.

## 2. Product Strategist

Read [the Product Strategist method](../skills/product-strategist/SKILL.md). Choose the target outcome and evaluate feasible approaches.

**Carry forward:** A product direction with success measures and tradeoffs.

**Check:** The chosen approach addresses the evidence and states what would change the decision.

## 3. Roadmap Communicator

Read [the Roadmap Communicator method](../skills/roadmap-communicator/SKILL.md). Sequence the chosen work by dependency, capacity, and learning value.

**Carry forward:** A roadmap with outcomes, owners, and review dates.

**Check:** The sequence is feasible and each item advances a stated outcome.

## Check the finished work

- Sources, dates, and uncertainty are visible for consequential findings.
- The chosen approach addresses the evidence and states what would change the decision.
- The sequence is feasible and each item advances a stated outcome.

## Finish

A roadmap with outcomes, owners, and review dates. The sequence is feasible and each item advances a stated outcome.
