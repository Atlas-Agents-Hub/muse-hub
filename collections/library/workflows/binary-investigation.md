# Investigate a Binary or Protocol

Explain observed software behavior and turn findings into practical defensive action.

## Bring these inputs

- Authorized binary or protocol sample
- Analysis question and isolated environment

## 1. Forensics

Read [the Forensics method](../skills/forensics-pro/SKILL.md). Preserve the sample identity and collect the initial evidence.

**Carry forward:** A hashed sample record and observation timeline.

**Check:** The evidence can be traced to the supplied sample and handling steps.

## 2. Binary and Protocol Analysis

Read [the Binary and Protocol Analysis method](../skills/reverse-engineer/SKILL.md). Use static and bounded dynamic analysis to answer the specific question.

**Carry forward:** Annotated behavior, structures, and confirmed hypotheses.

**Check:** Critical conclusions are supported by observable behavior or instruction-level evidence.

## 3. Vulnerability Management

Read [the Vulnerability Management method](../skills/vulnerability-management/SKILL.md). Assess any confirmed weakness and define remediation and retest.

**Carry forward:** A finding with affected versions, impact, fix, and retest procedure.

**Check:** The report supports a practical defensive decision without overstating untested impact.

## Check the finished work

- The evidence can be traced to the supplied sample and handling steps.
- Critical conclusions are supported by observable behavior or instruction-level evidence.
- The report supports a practical defensive decision without overstating untested impact.

## Finish

A finding with affected versions, impact, fix, and retest procedure. The report supports a practical defensive decision without overstating untested impact.
