# Reconcile and Review a Business Period

Connect reconciled accounts to a clear operational financial review.

## Bring these inputs

- General ledger and bank statement exports
- Reporting period, policies, and operating metrics

## 1. Month End Close

Read [the Month End Close method](../skills/month-end-close/SKILL.md). Reconcile the period and classify timing items and required corrections.

**Carry forward:** A reconciliation with explained differences and sign-off items.

**Check:** Opening balance, movements, and closing balance reconcile to the source records.

## 2. Financial Analyst

Read [the Financial Analyst method](../skills/financial-analyst/SKILL.md). Analyze the reconciled statements and explain material movements.

**Carry forward:** A financial review with calculation inputs and assumptions.

**Check:** Ratios and comparisons use consistent periods and reconciled figures.

## 3. Saas Metrics Coach

Read [the Saas Metrics Coach method](../skills/saas-metrics-coach/SKILL.md). Connect the financial review to the applicable operating metrics.

**Carry forward:** A metric review with definitions and the next management questions.

**Check:** Metric definitions, cohorts, and denominators are explicit and consistent.

## Check the finished work

- Opening balance, movements, and closing balance reconcile to the source records.
- Ratios and comparisons use consistent periods and reconciled figures.
- Metric definitions, cohorts, and denominators are explicit and consistent.

## Finish

A metric review with definitions and the next management questions. Metric definitions, cohorts, and denominators are explicit and consistent.
