# Create and Measure a Brand Campaign

Turn a clear message into channel-ready creative and a measurable campaign.

## Bring these inputs

- Audience, offer, and brand references
- Channels, budget, and success measure

## 1. Brand Guidelines

Read [the Brand Guidelines method](../skills/brand-guidelines/SKILL.md). Define the message, visual rules, and tone for the audience.

**Carry forward:** A concise brand and campaign brief.

**Check:** The brief is specific enough to guide copy and design consistently.

## 2. Ad Creative

Read [the Ad Creative method](../skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md). Create channel-specific variants from the approved brief.

**Carry forward:** Creative variants with a stated audience and hypothesis.

**Check:** Variants follow channel requirements and differ in a testable way.

## 3. Campaign Analytics

Read [the Campaign Analytics method](../skills/campaign-analytics/SKILL.md). Define measurement and compare observed outcomes against the hypotheses.

**Carry forward:** A campaign measurement plan and evidence-based iteration decision.

**Check:** Tracking and denominators are consistent and results are not attributed beyond the evidence.

## Check the finished work

- The brief is specific enough to guide copy and design consistently.
- Variants follow channel requirements and differ in a testable way.
- Tracking and denominators are consistent and results are not attributed beyond the evidence.

## Finish

A campaign measurement plan and evidence-based iteration decision. Tracking and denominators are consistent and results are not attributed beyond the evidence.
