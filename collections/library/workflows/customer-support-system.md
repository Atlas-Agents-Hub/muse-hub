# Build a Customer Support System

Turn recurring support work into a usable service process and knowledge base.

## Bring these inputs

- Support requests and service commitments
- Team capacity and existing knowledge

## 1. Customer Success Manager

Read [the Customer Success Manager method](../skills/customer-success-manager/SKILL.md). Identify customer needs, recurring friction, and escalation expectations.

**Carry forward:** A customer outcome and escalation map.

**Check:** The map reflects actual support evidence and service commitments.

## 2. Support Ops

Read [the Support Ops method](../skills/support-ops/SKILL.md). Design routing, coverage, and response measures for the mapped needs.

**Carry forward:** A support operating plan with ownership and thresholds.

**Check:** Coverage fits capacity and escalation rules handle urgent or uncertain cases.

## 3. Knowledge Ops

Read [the Knowledge Ops method](../skills/knowledge-ops/SKILL.md). Turn resolved cases into searchable, maintained support procedures.

**Carry forward:** A knowledge base with owners, review dates, and feedback paths.

**Check:** A new team member can find and follow a representative procedure.

## Check the finished work

- The map reflects actual support evidence and service commitments.
- Coverage fits capacity and escalation rules handle urgent or uncertain cases.
- A new team member can find and follow a representative procedure.

## Finish

A knowledge base with owners, review dates, and feedback paths. A new team member can find and follow a representative procedure.
