# Build a Cited Research Brief

Turn a defined question into a defensible explanation with a reproducible search trail.

## Bring these inputs

- Research question and decision
- Scope, date range, and available sources

## 1. Literature Reviews

Read [the Literature Reviews method](../skills/litreview/SKILL.md). Search the question and record inclusion criteria, source dates, and findings.

**Carry forward:** An included-source table with stable identifiers and exclusions.

**Check:** Every included claim has an inspectable source and meets the stated criteria.

## 2. Qualitative Analysis

Read [the Qualitative Analysis method](../skills/qualitative-analysis/SKILL.md). Code the included-source table for themes and conflicting findings.

**Carry forward:** A theme matrix with source excerpts and alternative explanations.

**Check:** Themes can be traced to source passages; disagreement is retained.

## 3. Scientific Argumentation

Read [the Scientific Argumentation method](../skills/scientific-argumentation/SKILL.md). Use the theme matrix to write the brief with claim-level support.

**Carry forward:** A cited brief, uncertainty statement, and next evidence question.

**Check:** The conclusion follows from the cited evidence and does not exceed the search scope.

## Check the finished work

- Every included claim has an inspectable source and meets the stated criteria.
- Themes can be traced to source passages; disagreement is retained.
- The conclusion follows from the cited evidence and does not exceed the search scope.

## Finish

A cited brief, uncertainty statement, and next evidence question. The conclusion follows from the cited evidence and does not exceed the search scope.
