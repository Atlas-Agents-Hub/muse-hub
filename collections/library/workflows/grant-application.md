# Develop a Grant Application

Connect a research aim to a feasible work plan, evidence, and justified budget.

## Bring these inputs

- Funding call and eligibility
- Research idea, team capacity, and cost inputs

## 1. Specific Aims Page

Read [the Specific Aims Page method](../skills/specific-aims-page/SKILL.md). Write aims against the funding call and define measurable success.

**Carry forward:** An aims page with hypotheses, outcomes, and alternatives.

**Check:** Aims are distinct, testable, and aligned with the call.

## 2. Grant Story Arc

Read [the Grant Story Arc method](../skills/grant-story-arc/SKILL.md). Turn the aims into a coherent need, approach, and impact narrative.

**Carry forward:** A narrative outline with evidence and reviewer questions.

**Check:** Each proposed activity supports an aim and has a plausible outcome.

## 3. Budget Justification

Read [the Budget Justification method](../skills/budget-justification/SKILL.md). Cost the activities using actual staffing, equipment, and timeline assumptions.

**Carry forward:** A line-item budget and narrative justification.

**Check:** Totals reconcile and each cost traces to an activity and applicable funder rule.

## Check the finished work

- Aims are distinct, testable, and aligned with the call.
- Each proposed activity supports an aim and has a plausible outcome.
- Totals reconcile and each cost traces to an activity and applicable funder rule.

## Finish

A line-item budget and narrative justification. Totals reconcile and each cost traces to an activity and applicable funder rule.
