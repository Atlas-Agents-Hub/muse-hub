# Investigate and Recover from an Incident

Coordinate response, establish the cause, and improve the recovery procedure.

## Bring these inputs

- Incident symptoms and service context
- Logs, metrics, and response authority

## 1. Incident Commander

Read [the Incident Commander method](../skills/incident-commander/SKILL.md). Establish the timeline, impact, ownership, and immediate stabilization actions.

**Carry forward:** A shared incident record and response plan.

**Check:** Actions address observed impact and ownership is unambiguous.

## 2. Incident Response

Read [the Incident Response method](../skills/incident-response/SKILL.md). Investigate the evidence, contain the fault, and verify recovery.

**Carry forward:** A causal account with containment and recovery results.

**Check:** The recovery resolves the observed failure and related systems are checked.

## 3. Runbook Generator

Read [the Runbook Generator method](../skills/runbook-generator/SKILL.md). Turn the confirmed recovery into an operational procedure.

**Carry forward:** A tested runbook with symptoms, commands, checks, and escalation.

**Check:** Another operator can follow the procedure and recognize success or failure.

## Check the finished work

- Actions address observed impact and ownership is unambiguous.
- The recovery resolves the observed failure and related systems are checked.
- Another operator can follow the procedure and recognize success or failure.

## Finish

A tested runbook with symptoms, commands, checks, and escalation. Another operator can follow the procedure and recognize success or failure.
