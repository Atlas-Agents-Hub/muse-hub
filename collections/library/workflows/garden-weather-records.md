# Understand Local Growing Conditions

Combine site observations with weather records to support seasonal garden planning.

## Bring these inputs

- Site location and observation log
- Weather records and the planning period

## 1. Gis Workflows

Read [the Gis Workflows method](../skills/gis-workflows/SKILL.md). Locate the site and align the observation records to a consistent spatial reference.

**Carry forward:** A site map and a location-checked observation table.

**Check:** Coordinates, dates, and spatial resolution match the intended site.

## 2. Climate Data Analysis

Read [the Climate Data Analysis method](../skills/climate-data-analysis/SKILL.md). Summarize local temperature, precipitation, and extremes for the planning period.

**Carry forward:** Seasonal summaries with units, missing-data notes, and historical context.

**Check:** Records cover the site and period; missing observations are handled explicitly.

## 3. Time Series Analysis

Read [the Time Series Analysis method](../skills/time-series-analysis/SKILL.md). Compare the garden observations with the seasonal summaries.

**Carry forward:** A trend and anomaly report for the next planning review.

**Check:** Time alignment is correct and associations are distinguished from causes.

## Check the finished work

- Coordinates, dates, and spatial resolution match the intended site.
- Records cover the site and period; missing observations are handled explicitly.
- Time alignment is correct and associations are distinguished from causes.

## Finish

A trend and anomaly report for the next planning review. Time alignment is correct and associations are distinguished from causes.
