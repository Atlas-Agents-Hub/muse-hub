# Prepare a Research Paper

Develop the manuscript, figures, and abstract into a consistent submission set.

## Bring these inputs

- Study methods and results
- Target journal instructions

## 1. Paper Structure

Read [the Paper Structure method](../skills/paper-structure/SKILL.md). Map the study question, method, and results to the target article structure.

**Carry forward:** A section outline and evidence-to-section map.

**Check:** Every central result has a location and the structure follows the journal requirements.

## 2. Publication Figures

Read [the Publication Figures method](../skills/publication-figures/SKILL.md). Build figures from the mapped results with units, uncertainty, and captions.

**Carry forward:** Exported figures and a figure-data manifest.

**Check:** Plotted values match the source data and labels remain legible at publication size.

## 3. Abstract Writing

Read [the Abstract Writing method](../skills/abstract-writing/SKILL.md). Write the abstract from the final outline and figures.

**Carry forward:** A journal-length abstract with the reported numerical findings.

**Check:** Every abstract result appears in the manuscript and its wording preserves the study limits.

## 4. Peer Review Checklist

Read [the Peer Review Checklist method](../skills/peer-review-checklist/SKILL.md). Review the complete submission for methodological and reporting gaps.

**Carry forward:** A corrected submission set and resolved review checklist.

**Check:** Methods, results, figures, references, and required declarations agree.

## Check the finished work

- Every central result has a location and the structure follows the journal requirements.
- Plotted values match the source data and labels remain legible at publication size.
- Every abstract result appears in the manuscript and its wording preserves the study limits.
- Methods, results, figures, references, and required declarations agree.

## Finish

A corrected submission set and resolved review checklist. Methods, results, figures, references, and required declarations agree.
