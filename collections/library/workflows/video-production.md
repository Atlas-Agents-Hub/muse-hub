# Produce a Captioned Video

Take a scene plan through editing and a checked final export.

## Bring these inputs

- Video brief and source media
- Target format, duration, and audience

## 1. Cinematic Shot Prompting

Read [the Cinematic Shot Prompting method](../skills/cinematic-shot-prompting/SKILL.md). Describe shots, camera choices, and continuity from the brief.

**Carry forward:** A shot plan with required assets and scene transitions.

**Check:** Shots serve the narrative and can be produced with the available material.

## 2. Video Editor

Read [the Video Editor method](../skills/video-editor/SKILL.md). Assemble the sequence and refine pacing, sound, and continuity.

**Carry forward:** An edited video and export settings.

**Check:** The sequence preserves meaning and is free of unintended visual or audio discontinuities.

## 3. Subtitle

Read [the Subtitle method](../skills/subtitle-pro/SKILL.md). Add and verify captions against the final cut.

**Carry forward:** A captioned export and subtitle file.

**Check:** Timing, spelling, reading speed, and speaker changes match the audio.

## Check the finished work

- Shots serve the narrative and can be produced with the available material.
- The sequence preserves meaning and is free of unintended visual or audio discontinuities.
- Timing, spelling, reading speed, and speaker changes match the audio.

## Finish

A captioned export and subtitle file. Timing, spelling, reading speed, and speaker changes match the audio.
