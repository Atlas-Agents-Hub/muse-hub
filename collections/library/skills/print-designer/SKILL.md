# Print Designer

## Production brief

Collect the printer's specification sheet, final trim size, print process, quantity, paper stock, binding, finishing, and delivery deadline. Record the required color profile, PDF standard, bleed, safe area, effective image resolution, total ink limit, and font policy before laying out the document.

## Color and images

- Use the printer's requested color workflow and ICC profile. Some workflows accept tagged RGB and convert at the RIP; others require prepared CMYK or spot separations.
- Soft-proof with the intended output profile. Review out-of-gamut colors against the approved physical reference or contract proof; spot inks and extended-gamut processes may reproduce colors that ordinary process CMYK cannot.
- Check effective image resolution at the placed size. Around 300 pixels per inch is common for close-viewed continuous-tone offset work, but the printer's screening, process, artwork, and viewing distance determine the requirement.
- Follow the supplied total ink and black-generation limits. Use the printer's small-type guidance; avoid accidental multichannel black text and unintended overprint.

## Layout and handoff

1. Set trim, bleed, safe areas, page count, and binding margins from the job specification. Use imposition only when the printer requests it.
2. Place images at their final size and inspect crops, transparency, line art, and fine detail. Check stock, folds, gutters, and creep where applicable.
3. Use readable type for the intended audience and physical format. Verify font licenses and the printer's embedding or outline requirements.
4. Preflight missing links, effective resolution, color spaces, spot names, separations, overprint, transparency, and page geometry.
5. Export the requested PDF standard with the agreed profile and marks. Reopen the exported file and compare every page with the approved layout.
6. Review a physical proof when color, finish, stock, or binding is material. Resolve discrepancies and record approval before authorizing the production run.

## Deliverables and checks

Deliver the press PDF, production specification, proof approval, and any editable source/assets requested by the printer. Confirm trim and bleed boxes numerically; inspect the separations and ink coverage; verify page order and font handling; and compare the proof with the approved artwork. If the printer changes a requirement, regenerate and recheck the affected export.
