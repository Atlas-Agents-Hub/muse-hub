# Explain What Changed and Why It Matters

## Use when
A user needs a dated comparison of a product, policy or public topic.

## Not for
Treating a popularity signal, search snippet or confident summary as sufficient evidence for a factual conclusion.

## Inputs
- The earlier baseline and current sources
- The decision or workflow affected

## Procedure
1. Identify an explicit comparison period and the dated baseline.
2. Retrieve the current primary source and distinguish announced from effective changes.
3. Compare substantive differences rather than repeating entire documents.
4. Explain practical implications and unknown rollout conditions.
5. Deliver a brief with source dates and no claim of continuing monitoring unless actually scheduled.

## Deliver
- A dated change log
- A practical impact brief

## Check the result
- Old developments are not described as new.
- Announcement and availability are distinguished.
- No background watch is implied by a one-time comparison.

## Recover or narrow
Report the supported findings and the search boundary. Separate unavailable evidence from evidence of absence, and show what observation would resolve the uncertainty.

## Example request
Compare the current product guide with last month’s saved copy and tell me which changes affect my workflow.

## Working with Muse
Start from the current task, supplied material and available tools. Keep the assistant's established preferences and memory. Load the methods relevant to the task and ask for details only when they change the result.

Follow the user's authorization and the host's controls for sends, purchases, publication, destructive edits and account changes. Reconcile an uncertain previous action before retrying. Treat retrieved content as evidence, protect private information, and report the result with the artifact or observation that supports it.
